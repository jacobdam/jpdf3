## What went well
- **The review cap works:** before ADR 0010, PRs ran 6 to 15 rounds (#133, #135, #142, #151). After it, the
  maximum is 4 rounds plus the final check (5 PRs), and the 3 deferred issues that hid a blocker were all deferred before it.
- **Size:** 3 of 82 PRs (4%) went over 1500 added lines (retro 0001 target ≤ 20%).
- **Stability:** 0 reverts, 0 judge alarms, 0 flaky tests; main's only red runs were 2 macOS-runner infra failures.
- **Scheduling is code now:** `cargo xtask next-session` (PRs #269, #272, #274) found this retro's incident trigger,
  which sessions applying rule h by hand had missed for about 30 hours (the third `blocked` label was put on
  #187 at 2026-11-22 14:58 UTC).

## What went badly
- **Records churn:** 20 of 82 merges (24%) changed only the journal, STATUS or LESSONS, and they took 28 review
  rounds. On 2026-11-23, 4 of 13 sessions did nothing but merge journal/STATUS conflicts, and each of those merges
  added a journal entry and a STATUS rewrite, which set up the next conflict.
- **Little product work:** 19 of 82 merges (23%) touched product paths. M0's open count fell only from 31 to 28 in
  2.8 days, though 42 issues closed.
- **Rework is still high:** 47% of reviewed PRs needed 3 or more rounds (retro 0001 target ≤ 35%; it was 58%).
  11 reached 4 rounds. Most of them were filters, gates, parsers or process rules: each round found another case
  that a broken version still passes (#183, #227, #234, #239, #241, #264, #265, #267; audits 0004–0006 each found one more).
- **Audit lag by hand:** the audits came after 12 to 22 merges, never at 10.

## Root causes (5 whys)

**1. Records churn.** Why did 4 sessions only resolve conflicts? Two open PRs both changed the end of
`ops/journal/2026-11-23.md` and rewrote `ops/STATUS.md`. Why do they both? TICK §5 has every session append to the
day's journal and rewrite STATUS, including sessions whose only work is a rerun or a conflict merge. Why does that
collide? Auto-merge lands after the session exits, so the next session branches from a main without it. Why does it
cascade? The session that merges the conflict writes its own journal entry and STATUS rewrite into the same PR.
**Root:** the per-session record is written into shared files, so any two records PRs conflict.

**2. Checkers and rules don't converge.** Why did 11 PRs reach 4 rounds? Each round found another input that a
broken filter, parser or gate still accepts (a near-miss case, a line shape, a run that checked nothing), or another
state of a process loop. Why didn't the author find them first? Retro 0001's template self-check asks "what broken
implementation still passes?" for conformance suites only. Why weren't the LESSONS entries enough? They were there
(each recurred 3+ times), but LESSONS is read at the start of a session, hours before the harness is written.
**Root:** same as retro 0001's root 2, for a kind of code the template doesn't name.

**3. Little product work.** Why only 23% product merges? Owner-requested process work (ADR 0010, ADR 0011, the owner
comment rules) came in through rule a and `human`, each process PR spawned proposals (18) and records PRs (20), and 5
audits ran. Why didn't anything push back? Nothing measures or budgets the work mix. **Root:** there is no work-mix
budget; ADR 0011 task 2 (the Planner, ≥ 70% of sessions on milestone work) is that fix, so this retro adds no
separate item and records 23% as the baseline. Action 1 removes part of the records share.
