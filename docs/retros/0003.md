## Root causes (5 whys)

**1. Little product work.** Why 4%? Of the 124 merges, 118 built or recorded the autonomy system: ADR 0011's
session types, the owner's process directions (#386, #398, #461, #466, #478, #508, #522), 9 ADRs and their TICK
text. Why did that work fill the milestone? It sat in M0, M0's gate needed it, and the Planner's work-mix budget
(ADR 0011 §3) only orders the queue; it never blocks, and M0's PLAN row had no product issue left to add. Why did
nothing move it out? Until ADR 0019 (#493, text in #533, merged today) a process issue chose the current milestone
by default. **Root:** process work had no milestone of its own that the exit path didn't need. ADR 0019 and `Ops`
are that fix. M1's 29 open issues are 15 product, 6 product-area tooling (`xtask/`, `tools/arlington/`) and 8
process: #508, #526 (term 1, M1's close stage), #511, #512 (term 4, the owner's "M1 for all 11" on PR #465),
#442 (term 1, before M1 admits #446's dependencies), and #527, #308, #248, for which no term is evident (#527 is M0's remaining
checklist; ADR 0012 §3 moved #308 and #248 to M1 before ADR 0019): those three are the Planner's to
check under §9 step 2, not this retro's (P7). This retro adds no item for it and records 4% as the M1 baseline
(Other decisions).

**2. Idle sessions are many, and each costs the fixed context.** Why 169 k tokens for an `idle` exit? 153 of the 271
idle sessions made only 2 tool calls, so the cost is the prompt itself (TICK, CLAUDE.md, the system prompt) carried
over the turns; reading LESSONS first adds about 100 k (118 sessions). Why so many? The driver starts a session every
couple of minutes whatever the last pick was, and on 11-27 and 11-28 no work was ready: issues waited for the
Planner's next day (ADR 0015), for the owner, or for a design window. **Root:** nothing slows the loop after an
`idle` pick. That is the driver's (`ops/driver/`, protected), so it goes to #539 (`Ops`) as a proposal, not to an
action item here; moving the scheduler first in TICK §1 would save at most the 118 sessions' extra 100 k and would
skip §1's `owner-todo` check, so this retro doesn't change §1.

**3. Rules over GitHub state draw one missed case per round.** Why did #479, #524, #528 and #530 reach the cap? Each
round's reviewer walked one more state the author had not tabulated (a feed kind's lifetime, a same-second close, a
reopen, a PR closing unmerged). Why weren't they tabulated? The template's self-check asks for a state table for
loop-bounding rules, but these are counts and filters over GitHub events, and the four LESSONS entries that said
"tabulate before round 1" (PRs #289, #312, #479, #524, #528) were read hours before the rule was written. **Root:**
same as retro 0002's root 2, for rules over GitHub state, which no checklist names. Action 1.
